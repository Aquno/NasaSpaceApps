- Elevations: 3D vectors on a sphere, not clamped at 0. Azimuth as JPL Horizons (N=0, E=90, clockwise).
- Edge rule: a ray stops where it leaves the DEM; terrain beyond is treated as unknown.
- Sanity: contour must stay within [-10, 35] deg and must not be constant.
